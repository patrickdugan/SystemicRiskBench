Reviewed all 15 crypto cases for direct hindsight leakage into stage0, source support and date-anchor ambiguity. All stage0 observations are explicitly synthetic counterfactual setups; none discloses later fraud findings, recoveries or eventual governance rescues as known facts.

## Remaining interpretation limits

- Other historical stage1 cutoffs are coarse reconstructed boundaries, not certified publication timestamps. Retain that disclosure and require an archive/hash pass before calling the track a strict point-in-time replay.
- Stage0 descriptions cue the failure mechanism because they are designed reasoning problems. They support causal inference and stress reasoning, but cannot alone demonstrate prospective crisis detection. Add near misses, non-crisis controls and structural lookalikes before interpreting a high score as forecasting skill.
- Case-level numerical proxies are hypothetical stress inputs, not observations at the historical cutoff. The parent exporter strips these numerical blocks; any future quantitative packet should identify when and why hypothetical stress values are supplied.
- The Kaiko card covers a February 22-23 multi-venue liquidity event. It does not claim every venue experienced identical liquidity loss. The FTX s1 applies to international-platform access; the Chicago Fed explicitly distinguishes the later US-platform pause.
- Complaints and indictments remain attributed allegations. The Mango card avoids using the stale 2024 conviction release as a claim about the present criminal-case disposition.
- Source titles, URLs, outcome anchors, mechanism labels and dated event names are evaluator-only because they can reveal memorized outcomes. Identity masking cannot remove historical pretraining.
